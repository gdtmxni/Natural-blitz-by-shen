Booking, step by step

Five steps to slay-ready

Booking online takes a few minutes. Here's exactly how it goes.

01

Choose your care

Pick wash, condition, detangle, dry & style, or the Full Blitz. Tell us how many wigs, the hair type and the length.

02

Pick your finish

Choose a style from our looks or upload an inspo photo, and let us know if it’s for a special occasion.

03

Pick a day

Choose an open day and time slot, then drop your wig off or have us pick it up.

04

Your details

Add your name and a 10-digit phone or WhatsApp number so Shen can reach you.

05

Secure it

Pay your deposit to lock in your slot. It goes toward your final balance.

Then we Blitz

Your wig is washed, conditioned, detangled, dried and styled with gentle, careful hands, then it comes back to you fresh, silky and ready to wear.

Please read before booking

Booking policies

Appointments

  • All appointments must be booked in advance.
  • Please make sure you select the correct service when booking.
  • Not sure? Contact us before confirming your appointment.
  • No extra guests unless approved.

Deposits

  • A non-refundable deposit is required to secure all bookings.
  • Deposits must be paid by bank transfer within 24 hours of booking.
  • No deposit, no appointment secured.
  • Your deposit goes toward your final balance.

Cancellations & rescheduling

  • Appointments must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 to 48 hours before.
  • Deposits can be transferred once only to a new date.
  • Same-day cancellations forfeit the deposit.
  • No-shows lose their deposit and may be blocked from future bookings.

Late policy

  • A 15-minute grace period is allowed.
  • After 15 minutes, a late fee of J$1,000 applies.
  • After 20 to 30 minutes, your appointment may be cancelled.
